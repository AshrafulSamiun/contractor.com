# Canva-Style Layout Idea for the Facebook Ad Image

## Layout Structure

### 1. Background

Use a clean professional background in dark navy, slate blue, or charcoal gray.
Add subtle construction-style pattern or blurred building silhouettes.

### 2. Left Side: Dashboard Mockup

Place a large dashboard preview card showing:

- Job Orders panel
- Staff workflow list
- Vehicle management section
- Reports or invoices summary

### 3. Right Side: Main Text

Headline in large bold text:
Manage Your Contractor Business Better

Subheadline below:
Jobs • Teams • Vehicles • Invoices

### 4. Lower Banner Strip

Add a highlight box with:

- One platform
- Real-time tracking
- Better decision making

### 5. CTA Button

Place a bright orange or green CTA button at the bottom right:
Book a Demo

### 6. Small Logo/Text

Top left: Contractor.com
Small trust text below or above:
Built for contractors and field service businesses

---

## Suggested Canva Design Settings

- Canvas size: 1080 x 1350 (Facebook portrait ad)
- Font style: modern bold sans-serif
- Headline font: 70-90 px
- Subheadline: 28-36 px
- Body text: 22-28 px
- CTA button: rounded rectangle with shadow
- Use white text for contrast against dark background

---

## Visual Elements to Include

- Checklist icon
- Team icon
- Truck icon
- Calendar icon
- Dollar/invoice icon
- Report chart/analytics icon

- Navy #0F172A
